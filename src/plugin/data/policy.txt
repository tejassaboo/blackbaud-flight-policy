Blackbaud Professional Services Travel Policy

1. INTRODUCTION
PURPOSE
The transition from a manual, 20 th Century travel and expense reporting system to an automated, modern
environment requires Blackbaud to simplify and modernize our travel policy. With the establishment of this new
policy, Blackbaud seeks to provide guidelines to follow in order to protect the interests of both the employee and
Blackbaud. This document serves to clarify the means by which travel should be arranged and expensed, and
parameters that employees should understand and apply when making travel decisions.

In addition, the implementation of new travel tools will allow Blackbaud to more accurately aggregate our spend -
and thus negotiate better deals with our travel suppliers.

SCOPE
This policy applies to all employees of Blackbaud and its subsidiaries, anywhere in the world, regardless of whether
the travel is billable or non-billable.

GUIDING PRINCIPLES

It would be impossible for any policy to cover every conceivable expense situation. Therefore, we expect all
Blackbaud employees to bear in mind 3 principles as they incur and submit expenses:
• First, our clients have entrusted us with their resources. To sustain their trust, we treat our expenses -
billable or non-billable - in a manner in which our clients would find responsible and ethical.
• Second, upholding our core values is important. One of our Core Values is that we are Good Stewards of
our Re sources. Practically speaking, this means that we hold each other accountable for exercising
moderation in our spending, selecting the lowest cost travel methods that reasonably meet the business
objective, selecting reasonable and moderate locations for business meals, etc.
• Third, employee safety is always important. Thus when faced with a low cost alternative that doesn't feel
safe, employees should exercise appropriate judgment in selecting a reasonable alternative.

RESPONSIBILITY AND ENFORCEMENT
It is company policy to reimburse employees for ordinary, necessary and reasonable travel expenses when directly
connected with or pertaining to the transaction of company business.

The employee is responsible for understanding and applying the travel policy and his/her manager is responsible
for accurately reviewing expense reports for reasonability and compliance. Employees who do not follow our travel
policy will be subject to withholding of reimbursement and/or disciplinary action.

The Accounts Payable Department is responsible for ensuring that expenses reimbursed or paid for by the company
are in compliance with this Corporate Travel Policy.

Maintenance and updating of the Corporate Travel Policy are the responsibility of the Finance Department and will
include collaboration with Purchasing, Travel Personnel, and Human Resources. Any questions, concerns or
feedback regarding this travel polic y or the company's travel management program should be addressed to the
Travel Desk at travel@blackbaud.com.

3. INTERNATIONAL TRAVEL GUIDELINES
GENERAL
For Blackbaud travelers who are U.S. citizens, many of the below sections reference guidance provided by the U.S.
State Department or U.S. Commerce Department. Please note the following websites for those t wo government
departments:

U.S. Department of State: http://travel.state.gov/travel/
U.S. Department of Commerce: http://www.trade.gov/cs/

For Blackbaud travelers who are non-U.S. Citizens, your home country provides identical services and information
through similar ministries or departments. Please consult tho se resources directly, or contact the Travel Desk for
assistance.

IMMUNIZATION
All employees who travel outside of his/her resident country on business are required to take the appropriate
immunization shots as recommended by the U.S. State Department , his/her home country health authorities and
local health authorities. The Travel Desk can advise you of the shots needed for various countries. For travel to
countries where extra immunizations are required, it is recommended you travel with a current copy of your
inoculation certificates and keep a copy at your desk so that they can be retrieved by a co -worker at your request.

CURRENCY EXCHANGE RATES
Travelers may use the rate published at oanda.com or the currency exchange location where the charges were
made for any cash expenditure. The Corporate Travel Credit Card will automatically translate expenditures placed
on the card into the card's based currency inclusive of any exchange fees.

LOCAL CURRENCY FEES
Travelers will be reimbursed for ATM/exchange fees incurred as a result of withdrawing funds in a currency other
than their own national currency. For example, a Sydney -based Blackbaud empl oyee traveling to the United
Kingdom might withdraw £100 from a Lloyd's Bank ATM machine at Heathrow Airport upon arrival to pay for the
Underground and other incidental expenses, and incur bank and alternate currency fees of £2.50. Those fees

represent a reimbursable expense. As a guide, travelers should plan for one withdrawal per day, unless multiple
currency zones are visited in the same day.

RESTRICTED/PROHIBITED COUNTRIES OR REGIONS
The U.S. State Department maintains an alert list of countries or regions where travel is prohibited, or where there
are travel warnings and travels should use caution. These apply to all Blackbaud travelers regardless of nationality.
Employees will not travel on business to prohibited countries; if visits to such areas seem necessary, CEO is
required. For travel to countries with warnings or alerts, Vice President approval is required, and travelers should
follow all relevant traveler safety guidelines.

CUSTOMS
SALEABLE MERCHANDISE
Employees should not carr y saleable merchandise including spare parts and software CD's. Should these be
necessary for the purpose of the trip, arrangements should be made to ship these items in advance. Otherwise,
large duties may be levied or goods may be confiscated. Your ho st or the Commerce Department website
referenced above can usually advise about such requirements in advance of travel.

PROHIBITED ITEMS
Some countries prohibit the importation of items such as alcoholic beverages and religious or pornographic
materials. All Blackbaud travelers can check with the U.S. State Department for information on specific countries
at the State Department website referenced above.

PASSPORTS AND VISAS
POLICY
It is the employee's responsibility to obtain any required documentation for travel to foreign countries. Blackbaud
will reimburse employees for Passports and Visas if an employee does not already have a passport and/or visa and
it is required for business travel. You may contact the Travel Desk for assistance in obtaining passport information
and request forms.

It is recommended to keep a current copy of your passport/visa at your desk so that it can be retrieved by a co -
worker at your request. Pack a second copy in your luggage separately from the original documents.

PASSPORT SERVICES
Please refer to the State Department website referenced above or your own host country authorities.

VISA SERVICES
For visa services, please refer to the State Department website referenced above or your own host country
authorities.

MEDICATIONS
Carry copies of all prescriptions for medication and keep the medication in the original pharmacy containers.

4. AIR TRAVEL
POLICY
GENERAL
Air travel should be considered the last alternative after all other options to conduct business have been considered
(i.e. Video/Telecommunications conferencing, e-mail, or telephone). If the destination is under four hours of driving
time, consideration must be given to driving instead of flying.

Air reservations must be booked through Concur or via the Travel Desk. You may not book airfare directly outside
of Concur. If there are flight prices/options that you do not see in Concur, contact the Travel Desk to book them on
your behalf. Airfare booked directly by an employee outside of Concur may not be reimbursed.

Employees with Corporate Travel Credit Cards will book their air travel on that card. For all o ther employees, air
travel will be purchased using Blackbaud's corporate card.

Private aviation expenses (e.g., private jet) are not reimbursable without the pre -approval of the CFO.

CLASS OF SERVICE
DOMESTIC
Our policy is that domestic air purchases are coach class, regardless of flight time, and booked via nonrefundable
tickets. Individuals may upgrade with frequent flyer miles or at their own expense. Exception requests for medical
reasons may be submitted and approved by both the travel department and the appropriate Vice President.

INTERNATIONAL
International travel is to be by lowest possible airfare available. Any international travel exceeding 8 hours (whether
a single segment or multiple segments combined) can be booked in an upgradeable fare or, with the prior approval
of the traveler's Vice-President, in business class. Employees may utilize their own frequent flier miles to upgrade
fares, where applicable.

ADVANCE PURCHASE
Whenever possible , trips should be scheduled with a minimum 7 day, and preferably with a 14 -day advance
planning to maximize the opportunity to obtain lowest possible airfares.

LOWEST AIRFARE SELECTION
All airline tickets must be booked at the lowest available airfare as determined by Concur or the Travel Desk (subject
to the parameters defined in this section).

When booking with Concur, airfare options will be denoted by color scheme:

• Green will denote an airfare that is (1) within policy and (2) within an acc eptable cost range. There is no
approval necessary to select a Green fare.
• Yellow will denote an airfare that is (1) within policy but (2) above the lowest fare parameters. There is no
pre-approval necessary to select a Yellow fare; however, the traveler must be prepared to justify his/her
selection at the time of booking and to defend his/her selection at the point of manager approval.

If there are unique circumstances surrounding your travel and none of the itineraries displayed in Concur meet your
needs, contact the Travel Desk for assistance.

For informational purposes, the following criteria are among those utilized to determine lowest available airfare:
• Itineraries that include connecting flights are preferred where such itineraries result in the lowest fare, the
routing requires no more than one layover (involving a plane change) each way, and such layover is no
more than 2 hours
Time Window: departure/arrival is no more than 2 hours before or after requested
time.
• Nonrefundable tickets must always be booked.
• Full Y fares, or upgradeable fares, are not allowed to be booked.

PREFERED AIRLINES

Blackbaud has negotiated special rates with specific carriers as an integral part of our overall objective of reducing
business-related travel expenses. Travelers should use these preferred carriers whenever possible. T hese
discounts are pre-loaded into Concur and are automatically calculated when the reservation has been completed.

FREQUENT FLIER PROGRAMS
Traveling employees may retain frequent flyer program benefits and incorporate them into their personal profile s
and receive points and rewards appropriately. However, participation in these programs may not influence selection
that may result in any incremental cost to the company beyond the "lowest available airfare" as defined in this policy.

CHANGES
We understand that travel schedules may need to change due to unforeseen circumstances. When that occurs,
standby fees or fees resulting from changing return flights en-route on the same day may be reimbursed. However,
travelers may be asked to provide business justification and are expected to do so when requested.

If a traveler elects to offer her/his seat in exchange for a voucher, or receives a voucher due to traveler
inconvenience (e.g., cancelled flight), s/he can make use of that voucher as s/he deems appropriate.

DOUBLE-BOOKINGS
Double-bookings and back-to-back flights are prohibited.

CANCELLATIONS
All airlines require cancellation of flights PRIOR to ticketed departure time to receive a credit or refund. Most e -
tickets can be voided within 24 hours from the date the ticket was issued without incurri ng fees, but this request
must be done thru the Travel Desk only.

UNUSED AIRLINE TICKETS
Unused airline tickets are tracked directly thru Concur. When a flight has been cancelled the information is sent to
the individual's profile for future use. It is the responsibility of the employee to make sure to use these tickets for
future travel.

MISCELLANEOUS FEES
1. Seat Assignments are not reimbursable, except for travel on airlines where pre -assignment of seats is not
possible (e.g., Southwest Airlines typically requires you to purchase your seat prior to travel to guarantee a
seat on a booked fli ght). Travelers will not be reimbursed for any upgrade seat selections when regular
economy seats are available.
2. Wi-Fi in flight (GOGO)-is reimbursable ONLY for flights over 1.5 hours. (Travelers should seek to use on an
as-needed basis.)
3. Checked bags are reimbursable up to 2 bags but will not be reimbursed for overweight bags.

The only exception to this is when booking rooms at the Charleston Courtyard by Marriott near Blackbaud

Hotel reservations must be booked by the employee through Concur or by the Travel Desk on behalf of the

Travelers should contact the travel desk or the after hour's number if a suitable hotel cannot be booked within the

Employees are entitled to stay in a single occupancy room with a private bath and may only accept room upgrades
to suites or executive floor rooms if the upgrade is at no additional cost to Blackbaud.

CONFERENCE/EVENT HOTELS
Concur will show discounted rates for conference and other special event hotels. In the event Concur does not

CANCELLATION
In the event a hotel room is no longer needed, advise the hotel or hotel chain directly to avoid any "no show" billing

effort should be made to avoid paying for hotel rooms that are not used.

FREQUENT GUEST PROGRAMS
Many hotels have frequent guest programs that reward travelers with free accommodations in exchange for
frequent patronage at the hotel. Awards from such programs may be retained by travelers for personal use.
Participation in these programs may not influence hotel selection which would result in additional cost beyond any
Blackbaud negotiated rate or other preferred rate. Any membership fees associated with joining these programs
are not reimbursable.

MINI-BAR/SNACKS
Mini-bar and snack expenses are only reimbursable when substituting for a meal.

INTERNATIONAL OVERNIGHT/MORNING ARRIVAL
For travelers on international flights of eight hours or greater where a business meeting occurs on the day of
arrival, it is permitted to use and expense airport arrival lounges. If no such facility is available, travelers may
book and expense a hotel room for the night coinciding with the day of travel in order to prepare for same day
meetings. Any arrival lounge/hotel room fees otherwise incurred will not be eligible for reimbursement.

BEST VALUE DECISIONS
Travelers often choose to stay with friends or relatives in destination city for comfort and for reasons of cost. In this
circumstance,
Blackbaud will reimburse travelers host expenses for one hospitality meal in lieu of these savings, up to 40% of the
amount of the USG per diem lodging. The Notes section of the submitted expense should clearly outline the
situation and the savings.
Example: John travels to New York in July and stays with his brother. The USG per diem lodging for NYC
is $216. They go out to dinner, and John's brother's portion of the bill is $100. Blackbaud reimburses
John for $86.40 (40% of $216) and John (or his brother) is responsible for $13.60. John (and his brother)
has saved Blackbaud nearly $130.
Travelers often choose low cost options which invoke incremental, incidental expenses. In this circumstance,
Blackbaud will reimburse travelers for incidental expenses of less than $20 per day assuming the low cost option
plus $20 is less than the standard option. The Notes section of the submitted expense should clearly outline the
situation.
Example: John travels to New York in July and is eligible to book a hotel with a gym for $216. He
identifies a suitable hotel for $125, but it has no gym. John pays $15 to a nearby health club for a daily
pass. His total lodging cost including gym was $140. Blackbaud reimburses John for $140, and saves
$76.

6. RENTAL CARS

POLICY
As explained in Section 2, employees with Corporate Travel Credit Cards are expected to pay all business -related
rental car fees on that card.

Cars should be rented by employees when other means of transportation are unavailable, more costly, or
impractical.
• Internationally, and in large domestic cities, public transportation and taxis are often more convenient and
cost efficient and are therefo re the preferred alternative. When traveling internationally, it is strongly
recommended that cars are not rented.
• Employees should share rides when travel plans allow.

• Fines, citations, or other charges that are the result of improperly operating or parking the rental vehicle are
not reimbursable by the Company.

Travelers are not reimbursed for fees associated with GPS devices or other accessories.

INSURANCE
All employees using rental automobiles (or personal car) for business purposes are expected to review the Driver's
Safety Training video available on the Travel page on Meebee.
(http://meebee/BBSites/BBTravelers/Pages/BBTravelers.aspx )

U.S. EMPLOYEES
The Corporate Travel Credit Card provides insurance for damage to or loss of a rental automobile due to a collision,
fire, theft, or vandalism, even for loss of use, towing expenses, and items that are not permanently installed in the
vehicle, but itemized in the rental agreement. If the employee purchases additional coverage, it is not reimbursable.

The employee must ensure that the "Blackbaud, Inc." name also appears on the rental agreement. This will ensure
the Blackbaud's insurance coverage will be valid in certain instances. This should automatically occur through
Concur, but it is the employee's responsibility to verify this at the time of rental.

Non-US and CANADA EMPLOYEES
Third-party liability insurance needs to be purchased from the rental car agency if tr aveling outside of your country
of employment.

SIZE LIMIT/EXTRA AMMENITIES
All employees are expected to book Intermediate or Mid -sized cars. Blackbaud will not reimburse for larger size
vehicles unless as specified below, in Section E "Upgrades". GPS navigation systems and satellite radio are not
reimbursable.

UPGRADES
Travelers may book a category one-level higher only if one of the following applies:
1. When two or more Blackbaud employees are traveling together
2. When entertaining customers
3. When cars in an authorized category are not available
4. When the employee can be upgraded at no extra cost
5. When transporting excess baggage such as Company equipment

Travelers should state which of the above applies when submitting expenses in Concur.

If a traveler is upgraded by the rental car company at no additional cost (i.e., a full size car at an intermediate rate),
s/he will be reimbursed at the allowable rate.

1 INTRODUCTION

With the establishment of this policy, Blackbaud seeks to promote a safe driving culture by keeping our employees and
other drivers as safe as possible, while reducing the likelihood of accidents and such related costs. This policy applies to
all employees of Blackbaud, located in North America, regardless of whether the Employee is driving a company vehicle, a
rental car, or a personal automobile while in the course of company business. It is a requirement for all employees to follow
safe driving practices, defensive driving principles, and driving regulations.

2 BLACKBAUD RESPONSIBILITIES

Blackbaud requires staff to drive under safe conditions. We will do this by:

• Maintaining adequate insurance coverage;
• Ensuring that staff are aware of their responsibility to check their vehicles prior to use;
• Ensuring that where additional training is required, it will be provided;
• Collecting statistics on accidents to ensure continuous improvement of our driving policy; and
• Annually reviewing Black baud Driving Policy to ensure the policies are adequately developed and provide for the
highest safety of our employees and others on the road.

Any questions or concerns regarding this driving policy should be addressed to Human Resources.

3 EMPLOYEE RESPONSIBILITIES

Safe driving is an essential job requirement for all Blackbaud employees. Each employee is responsible for complying with
the policies and procedures listed in this manual. Failure to comply with any aspect of this Blackbaud Driving Policy will
result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment.

All employees who may drive in the course of Blackbaud business must have a current and valid driver's license.

Drivers with more than six (6) points on their driving record may not drive in the furtherance of company business. It is the
Employee's responsibility to inform his supervisor and human resources in writing if he exceeds the six point allowance.
Once the Employee's driving record returns to conformity with this policy, he should again inform his supervisor and human
resources in writing of his record's improvement and his ability to drive for company business. In the event that the abilit y
to drive is a requirement for an Employee's job, if his driving record exceeds six (6) points, he may face disciplinary actions,
up to and including termination of employment.

A. Training

All employees who will drive on company business are required to watch the Driver's Safety video upon hire. Additionally,
if an Employee is found at-fault in an accident or traffic incident, then he will be required to go through Remediation Training
by re-watching the video and then informing his supervisor and The General Counsel's Office of his completion of this
requirement.

The Driver's Safety Video is located at http://meebee/BBSites/BBTravelers/Pages/BBTravelers.aspx.

B. When an Employee is on Company Business

This policy does not just apply to those employees who are regular travelers. There are many scenarios where an Employee
can be driving his own vehicle and be considered driving in the "course and scope of his employment". The following are a
short list of examples where an Employee is driving in the course of his employment, and is therefore required to follow the
prescripts of the Blackbaud Driving Policy. This list is not intended to be all inclusive nor exhaustive.

• An Employee driving to visit a customer location.
• An Employee driving a rental or personal car to a meeting.
• An Employee driving either a company vehicle or a personal car to pick up office supplies.
• An on-call Employee traveling back and forth to work.
• An Employee is out picking up lunch for a team event.

C. Driving Techniques and Requirements

The list below sets forth the driving requirements for any Employee who is driving in the course of Blackbaud business.
This list is not exhaustive, and employees are encouraged to adopt additional driving practices that would make their drive
safer. Any Employee who fails to follow these requirements will be subject to appropriate disciplinary action. Continuous
disregard for these rules will lead to further disciplinary action, up to and including termination. Employees who drive on
company business must:

• Maintain a current and valid driver's license;
• Immediately notify their manager if their driver's license has been suspended, cancelled, or has limitations placed
on it;
Stop driving when tired;
• Assess driving hazards and anticipate "what if scenarios";
• Follow all road signs, signals, and roadway markings;
• Be conscious of adverse weather conditions and follow careful traffic practices;
• Wear a safety belt and require passengers to do the same;
• Drive within the legal and posted speed limits;
• Comply with traffic legislation;
• Always drive with the vehicle headlights on at night or in adverse weather conditions;
• Do not use a mobile phone or any wireless device when driving - See instructions for cell phone use;
• Check vehicle prior to the journey to ensure that vehicles are within safe operating conditions;
• Take regular and adequate rest breaks;
• Plan journey ahead, taking into consideration pre -journey work duties, the length of the trip and post journey
commitments;
Take a taxi, train, or bus when returning from long haul flights; and
Ensure
familiarity with the vehicle to be driven.

D. Impaired Driving

An Employee may not operate a motor vehicle at any time when his ability is impaired, affected, or influenced by alcohol,
illegal drugs, medication, illness, fatigue, or injury. If an Employee is unable to drive due to his impaired state, he must not
drive and inform his supervisor as necessary.

Any Employee convicted of dr iving under the influence of alcohol or illegal drugs or medication in the year prior to
employment or after employment may be ineligible to drive in furtherance of company business.

If an Employee is convicted of driving under the influence of alcohol or illegal drugs or medication while driving in furtherance
of company business, the Employee will face disciplinary actions, up to and including termination of employment.

E. Driving a Company Vehicle

Blackbaud has two company vehicles on site at the Daniel Island Headquarters. When driving a company vehicle,
employees must follow the same rules and regulations as listed in Driving Techniques and Requirements above and adhere
to the policies outlined in Facilities for checking out the vehicle.

Also, any citations received while driving must be taken care of promptly by the driver. It is the driver's responsibility t o
inform his manager and the Director of Facilities of any citation received while driving the company vehicle. If a drunk or
reckless driving citation is received, the driver will be disqualified from continued use of a Company automobile and may
receive corrective action, up to and including termination.

Company vehicles are for use in furthering business purposes only. Only those authorized to drive the vehicle can remove
the vehicle from the Blackbaud parking lot. Any unauthorized use of the vehicle will result in disciplinary action, up to an d
including termination. Additiona lly, if an accident occurs while an unauthorized person is driving the vehicle, the
unauthorized driver may be required to make restitution for the physical damages to the company vehicle, as well as assume
all responsibility for injury to persons and damage to property as a result of the incident.

F. Driving a Rental Vehicle on Company Business

When driving a rental car, employees are advised to follow the Driving Techniques and Requirements as listed above. It is
required that when renting a vehicle, the Employee uses his company credit card. The reason this is the preferred method
of payment is because the corporate credit card provides insurance for damage to or loss of a rental automobile due to a
collision, fire, theft, or vandalism. Additionally, it covers the loss of use, towing expenses, and items that are not permanently
installed in the vehicle, but itemized in the rental agreement.

Further, employees must report any vehicle defects to the Rental Car Company as soon as they become aw are of the
problem.

G. Driving Personal Vehicles on Company Business

When an Employee drives his personal vehicle for business purposes he has a duty to maintain the vehicle and drive with
the same caution as is necessary with a rental or company car - See Driving Techniques and Requirements above.
Blackbaud will not accep t liability for any damage that is caused to a privately owned vehicle. Listed below are basic
guidelines for each Employee to follow:

• Employee must maintain liability coverage on his vehicle up to the limits required by his state's law;
• Employee must ensure that his insurance covers business travel;
• Employees may only drive legally registered vehicles on company business; and
• Employee must maintain current state vehicle inspections if the state requires one.

H. Wireless Device Use

Blackbaud employees are prohibited from using their mobile phone, Blackberry, or other wireless communications device
while operating a motor vehicle for company business, unless in a hands-free mode, or as otherwise regulated by state law.

I. Using Navigation/GPS Units

While Blackbaud does not reimburse employees for the purchase or rental of a Navigation/GPS unit, they can be helpful to
prevent an Employee from getting lost. However, such units can serve as a major distraction and force the driver's attention
and eyes off the road. When using a Navigation/GPS Unit, the Employee must:

• Use extra caution when viewing the Navigation/GPS screen by making only short glances when the vehicle is
moving;
• Increase the following distance when viewing the screen and looking for street signs, addresses, etc.; and
• Only enter or change the destination address in the Navigation/GPS unit when the vehicle is at a complete stop.

4 POST-ACCIDENT PROCEDURES

If an Employee is involved in an accident, he should stop his vehicle at the scene or as close as is safe, always ensuring
his safety first. Regardless of the magnitude of the accident, contact the police immediately to inform them of the incident
and obtain a copy of the police report. The Employee must submit a copy of the police report to the Finance Department
within 10 days of the incident or within 5 days if there are any bodily injuries.

Additionally, never authorize repairs to the property of others or make commitments as to Blackbaud's responsibility.
